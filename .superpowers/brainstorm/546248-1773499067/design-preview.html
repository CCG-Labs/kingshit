What changes:

1. Terrain types
6 clusters → Mountain (gray raised)
6 clusters → Forest (dark green, dense trees)
3 clusters → Lake (blue, animated shimmer)

2. Edge blending
Tiles bordering a different terrain type
interpolate toward neighbor's color,
softening the hard rectangular cuts.

3. Worn paths
Any tile on the BFS flow field gets
a subtle dirt tint overlay — making the
enemy routes visually readable.

4. Decoration density
Forest: ~90% tree coverage
Mountain: ~70% rock coverage
Water: lily pad / ripple details
Open grass: sparse deco unchanged

5. Grass variation
Noise-based hue+lightness variation
gives open tiles organic color patches
instead of uniform flat green.
