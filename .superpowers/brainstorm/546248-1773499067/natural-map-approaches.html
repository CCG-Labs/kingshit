Making the Map Look More Natural

Which approach best captures the organic feel you're after?

Low effort

A — Terrain Variety

Give blocked tiles distinct visual identities: dark rocks for mountain clusters, blue-tinted tiles for lake areas, richer green for forest. The map stays grid-based but regions feel purposeful.

Medium effort

B — Worn Paths + Denser Deco

Add visual dirt paths where enemies actually walk (via flow field heat map), increase tree/rock coverage to ~90% of blocked tiles, and add more CA passes for smoother cluster edges.

High effort

C — Full Overhaul

All of the above plus: tile edge blending (transition tiles between terrain types), noise-based grass color variation with patches, and height-based shading to give the landscape elevation feel.
